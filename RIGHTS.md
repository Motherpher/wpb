# Rights and reuse

This repository documents and analyses published work by Weronika Pérez Borjas.

## Original works
Copyright in articles, academic texts, photographs, translations and other original published material remains with the relevant author, photographer, publisher or other rights holder.

The repository should not reproduce full copyrighted works merely because they are publicly accessible online.

## Repository material
Bibliographic metadata, research notes, taxonomies and repository-authored analysis are maintained separately from the underlying works.

No licence for repository-authored material is asserted here yet. The repository owner should choose one deliberately if the project later becomes an official or collaborative portfolio.

## Future portfolio
Before republishing excerpts, photographs, PDFs or scans, confirm the applicable rights and publisher terms.
