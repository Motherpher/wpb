# Writer dossier — Weronika Pérez Borjas

## Executive assessment
The recovered corpus is best understood as a progression from fashion/body scholarship through cultural journalism into transnational social reportage.

The most coherent through-line is not any single subject area. It is an interest in **classification and boundary conditions**: how bodies, identities and people are made legible by cultures and institutions, and what happens when lived reality exceeds those categories.

## Formation
Published academic records connect Pérez Borjas to Stockholm University and Fashion Studies. Early work on Rick Genest/Zombie Boy and Jean Paul Gaultier's anatomical *Flayed* costume treats the body as a visual and social system.

## Cultural expansion
The Forumist, Krull Magazine and Totally Stockholm broaden the field into music, art, Stockholm neighbourhoods, African and diasporic cultural production, spirituality, subculture and creative work.

## VICE transition
In 2016 cultural situations become increasingly explicit social field sites. The corpus includes Polish migration to Sweden, parent/child privacy on social media, family attitudes toward nudity, bodily suspension performance, Eurovision, race and Järva Pride.

## Long-form maturation
The 2018–2020 material is less lifestyle-led and more institutional:
- Swedish civil defence and fear of conflict
- nonbinary recognition and language
- nursing labour and healthcare capacity
- abortion law and cross-border access
- Cuban migration and detention

## Core method
The mature reporting often reveals large systems from below. Instead of explaining an institution first, it begins with a person trying to live through it.

## The Cuban reportage
*Pięciu Kubańczyków, cztery miesiące w niewoli* is central because the journalist becomes part of the information chain. Family members cannot locate detained relatives; authorities and diplomatic channels fail to provide a coherent account; the reporting process itself becomes evidence of institutional fragmentation.

The piece is therefore not only about migration. It is about the production, withholding and disappearance of information inside transnational systems.

## Writing modes
1. Academic/analytical
2. Cultural/essayistic
3. Social-observational
4. Long-form reportage

The development is cumulative rather than discontinuous: body → identity → classification → institution → rights/mobility/access.

## Working description
A concise evidence-based description of the mature corpus is:

> A transnational social and cultural reporter whose work repeatedly explores identity, migration and the points where lived experience meets institutional categories.

This remains a research interpretation and should not be represented as the writer's own self-description without confirmation.
