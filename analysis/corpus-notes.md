# Corpus notes

## Completeness
The corpus is reconstructed from surviving publisher pages, author archives, journal metadata and indexed publication records. It is not claimed to be exhaustive.

## Known archive problems
- older culture magazines have inconsistent author tagging
- VICE has migrated archive infrastructure
- individual articles can survive while author pages lose them
- print-only work may remain undiscovered
- some outlet pages expose bylines but not reliable dates

## Analytical caution
Frequency in the recovered corpus is not necessarily frequency in the writer's complete output. Do not turn archive survival into a claim about editorial priorities.

## Current strongest evidence period
2016 and 2018–2020 are comparatively well documented because VICE, Gazeta Wyborcza, Newsweek and Onet retain strong publication metadata.
