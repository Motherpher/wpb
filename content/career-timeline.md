# Career timeline — analytical reconstruction

## 2012–2014 — Body and fashion research
Research and publication focus on anatomy, tattooing, fashion, bodily difference, visual representation and the boundary between normality and deviance.

Key texts:
- *Zombie Boy: moda a ciało modela*
- *Turning the flesh inside-out...*

## 2014–2016 — Cultural journalism
The field expands into fashion, music, art, neighbourhoods, diaspora, spirituality and creative production. The Forumist, Krull Magazine and Totally Stockholm are central outlets in the recovered corpus.

## 2016 — Social-observational turn
VICE becomes a bridge between cultural journalism and social reportage. Recurring subjects include bodies, motherhood, Polish migration to Sweden, race, LGBT politics, national identity and subculture.

## 2018–2020 — Long-form social reportage
The recovered work becomes more institutionally focused:
- Swedish civil defence and Russia
- nonbinary identity, law and language
- Swedish nursing and healthcare labour
- abortion travel between Sweden and Poland
- Cuban migration, detention and border systems

The phases overlap. They are analytical tools rather than claims that the writer herself used these labels.
