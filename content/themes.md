# Recurring themes

## Body & representation
The body is the earliest organising subject: anatomy, fashion, tattooing, nakedness, bodily modification, gender and racial representation.

## Identity & categorisation
A recurring question is what happens when people do not fit available social, linguistic or administrative categories.

## Migration & belonging
Migration appears as lived movement, memory, family separation, diaspora and an encounter with institutions rather than merely a demographic category.

## Sweden–Poland comparison
Sweden and Poland repeatedly function as a comparative field for norms, migration, labour, healthcare, law and reproductive rights.

## Race, diaspora & cultural representation
The corpus includes work on Afro-Swedish art, African cultural production, anti-racism, nationalism and representation.

## Institutional seams
The mature reportage often starts from a person and reveals the system from below: border authorities, healthcare systems, legal gender categories, reproductive law or civil-defence institutions.

## Cuba
Cuba appears through cultural memory, Afro-Cuban spirituality, family reference and migration reporting. The archive does not infer identity beyond what published material explicitly establishes.
