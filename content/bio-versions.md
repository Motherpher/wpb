## Short
Weronika Pérez Borjas is a writer and reporter working across culture, identity, migration and social institutions. Her published work moves from fashion and visual-culture research through cultural journalism to long-form reporting on borders, gender, healthcare, labour and reproductive rights.

## Medium
Weronika Pérez Borjas is a writer and reporter whose work spans cultural criticism, interviews, social observation and long-form reportage. Beginning in fashion studies and research into the body, anatomy and representation, her writing expanded into music, art, diaspora and urban culture before moving increasingly toward migration, gender, healthcare, labour, reproductive rights, borders and public institutions. Across the corpus, a recurring strength is the ability to make large systems visible through individual experience.

## Portfolio positioning
Transnational social and cultural reporter working at the intersection of identity, migration, lived experience and institutional systems.
