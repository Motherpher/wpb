# Method and style

## Three broad writing modes

### Academic / analytical
Concept-driven writing grounded in visual and fashion theory. Objects and bodies are treated as social signs.

### Cultural magazine
More sensory, first-person and associative. Interviews and place-based observation become central.

### Reportage
Later work is structurally tighter: scene → person → dialogue → document/institution → return to person.

## Institutional systems seen from below
A repeated mature method is:

**individual experience → concrete situation → institutional encounter → wider system → return to lived experience**

This can be seen particularly in work on nurses, nonbinary legal recognition, cross-border abortion access and the five Cuban migrants.

## Reporter positionality
First person is not used in a single way. In culture writing it can establish voice; in field reporting it establishes presence; in the Cuban reportage it documents the reporting process itself and the difficulty of obtaining information.

These are repository interpretations based on the recovered corpus.
