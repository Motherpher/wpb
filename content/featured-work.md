# Featured work — working selection

This is a research-driven starting set for a future portfolio. Final public selection should be made with the writer/rights holder.

## Pięciu Kubańczyków, cztery miesiące w niewoli — Onet, 2020
Migration, family, detention, borders and institutional information failure across several states. The strongest recovered example of participant/investigative long-form reportage.

## Szwedki przez lata przyjeżdżały po aborcję do Polski... — Newsweek Polska, 2020
Uses a historical reversal in abortion travel between Sweden and Poland to connect law, memory and reproductive rights.

## Szwedzkie pielęgniarki znikają... — Duży Format, 2020
Healthcare systems made visible through nurses' working conditions and labour choices.

## Nie ona i nie on... — Duży Format, 2019
Nonbinary lived experience confronting legal, administrative and grammatical categories.

## A jeśli nadejdą Rosjanie? Szwedzi szykują się na wojnę — Duży Format, 2018
Civil defence, public preparedness and Swedish security culture.

## Rainbows and Racism Marched Together in Sweden During LGBT Pride Week — VICE, 2016
A politically complex field report where LGBT rights, anti-racism and anti-immigration politics collide.

## Pytamy różne pokolenia Polaków na emigracji... — VICE Polska, 2016
An intergenerational migration piece that marks the transition toward later transnational social reporting.

## Turning the flesh inside-out... — Fashion, Style & Popular Culture, 2014
A useful research anchor for the body/representation lineage that persists through the later corpus.
