# Weronika Pérez Borjas — working writer profile

Weronika Pérez Borjas is a writer and reporter whose published work moves across cultural criticism, interviews, social observation and long-form reportage.

Her early work developed from fashion studies and research into the body, anatomy, visual culture and the construction of normality and difference. That foundation later widened into writing on music, art, subculture, diaspora and urban life, before moving increasingly toward migration, gender, labour, healthcare, reproductive rights, borders and public institutions.

A recurring strength of the work is cross-cultural translation. Poland and Sweden form one important axis; Cuba and wider transnational contexts form another. Rather than treating those settings as abstract national comparisons, the reporting frequently begins with individuals encountering systems in everyday life.

The mature reportage is particularly interested in institutional seams: the points where law, bureaucracy, professional systems or national borders fail to align cleanly with lived experience.

> Working analytical description: a transnational social and cultural reporter specialising in identity, migration and institutional boundary conditions.
