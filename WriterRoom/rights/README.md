# WriterRoom Rights & Attribution

Rights and attribution are tracked separately from authorship and portfolio selection.

## Minimum concerns

- who owns copyright;
- whether the work may be linked, excerpted or reproduced;
- image/photography rights;
- translation rights where relevant;
- attribution/credit requirements;
- embargo or publication restrictions;
- publisher/platform-specific terms where known.

## Rule

A verified publication record does not by itself grant permission to reproduce the full text, images or publisher assets.

WriterRoom should prefer metadata, links, approved excerpts and rights-cleared media on public surfaces.
