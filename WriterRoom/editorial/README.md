# WriterRoom Editorial Desk

The Editorial Desk is the review and development layer for active writing. It is separate from CareerHub and from the publication archive.

## Editorial functions

- form/function review;
- structural coherence;
- source/referential control;
- style/register calibration;
- deployment/readiness review;
- fact/claim verification support;
- revision history.

## Writer & the Beast / Publicist

`The Writer and the Beast` may be invoked as an optional editorial review protocol. It does not become the editor of record and does not overwrite authorial decisions.

Recommended review sequence:

1. intake/classification;
2. form and function;
3. structural coherence;
4. referential/source control;
5. style and affect;
6. deployment readiness.

## Human authority

Weronika retains authorship and editorial authority. AI/editorial protocol output is advisory unless she explicitly adopts a revision.

## Claim control

Where useful, factual claims should be traceable as:

`claim → source(s) → support level → verification status`.

Editorial analysis never turns an unsupported proposition into a verified fact.
