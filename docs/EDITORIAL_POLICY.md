# Editorial policy

## Purpose
WPB is a research archive first and a future portfolio-content system second.

## Source hierarchy
1. Original publisher page or journal record
2. Publisher author/archive page
3. Institutional catalogue or library record
4. Archived copy of original material
5. Secondary reporting or search result

## Copyright
Do not commit full copyrighted articles unless rights and permission are explicitly established. Store metadata, short summaries, analytical notes and links instead.

## Fact / interpretation separation
Biographical and bibliographic facts must be source-supported. Analytical claims must be written as interpretations of the corpus rather than facts about authorial intention.

## Personal information
Only store professional and publicly relevant biographical information required to understand the work. Do not infer or aggregate sensitive personal data.

## Corrections
Corrections should be made through Git history with a clear commit message. Do not silently rewrite uncertain records into certainty.

## Portfolio mode
Future portfolio copy can be more concise and reader-facing than archive analysis, but it must derive from the same verified corpus.
